# KICD COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST — Senior School (CBC), Grade 10–12

**Status:** Core directive. Compliance is checked against the official **KICD Senior School Curriculum
Designs** (Grade 10, published June 2024), not against memory or against old 8-4-4 syllabuses.

**Primary sources** (verify against the current PDF before shipping a grade level):
- Grade 10 Chemistry — KICD Senior School Curriculum Design, ISBN 978-9914-52-913-5
  ([PDF](https://teacherske.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Grade-10-Chemistry-KICD-Curriculum-Designs.pdf))
- Grade 10 Biology — KICD Senior School Curriculum Design, ISBN 978-9914-52-915-9
  ([PDF](https://freeexams.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Biology-Grade-10-Curriculum-Designs.pdf))
- KICD Rationalised Curriculum Designs (all grades, check for the current year's revision)

> ⚠ **Version discipline.** KICD has been rationalising designs year on year. Every strand map in
> `disciplines/` must record **which design edition it was built from** (year + ISBN). When KICD
> releases a revision, re-run §9 on the affected sub-strands before writing another word.

## 1. The five-part spine

The KICD design organises **every** learning area into the same five columns. A lesson is not
compliant unless all five are explicitly present.

| # | Component | What KICD calls it | Our requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Strand** | The big theme (replaced "topic") | Named at the top of every chapter |
| 2 | **Sub-strand** | The unit within the strand | Named at the top of every lesson, with lesson count |
| 3 | **Specific Learning Outcomes** | "By the end of the sub-strand, the learner should be able to…" | Quoted in teacher metadata; every outcome must be teachable and assessable in our text |
| 4 | **Suggested Learning Experiences** | The activities | We must provide an equivalent *or better* activity, using locally available materials where the design says so |
| 5 | **Key Inquiry Question(s)** | The question that drives the sub-strand | Printed verbatim as the lesson's opening question |

**Non-negotiable:** if the design has 4 outcomes for a sub-strand, our lesson set covers **4**. Not 3,
and not 6 padded ones. Under-coverage fails an audit; over-coverage is off-syllabus teaching and is
also a fail.

---

## 2. Lesson architecture

From the Grade 10 designs:

- A lesson is **40 minutes**.
- Compulsory learning areas get **4 lessons per week**; optional/elective areas (Chemistry, Biology)
  get **6 lessons per week**.
- **180 lessons** per learning area per year, totalled across the strands.
- The suggested number of lessons per sub-strand "may be less or more depending on the context" —
  but the **year total must still land at 180**. If we rebalance, we must rebalance the total.

Our chapter spine (see `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §5) must fit inside 40 minutes of *learner* time, not writer time.

---

## 3. The seven Core Competencies

Every sub-strand must develop at least one, and it must be **genuine** — a competency named in the
metadata but not actually exercised by the activity is a compliance failure.

1. **Communication & Collaboration** — group work, presentations, peer explanation
2. **Critical Thinking & Problem Solving** — interpreting data, inferring, predicting, evaluating
3. **Creativity & Imagination** — modelling, designing, inventing
4. **Citizenship** — local and global awareness, community engagement
5. **Digital Literacy** — searching, simulating, using technology to learn
6. **Learning to Learn** — reflecting, independent practice, self-correction
7. **Self-Efficacy** — confident presentation, ownership of learning

**Target:** a book should touch **all seven** across a full grade, and each sub-strand should name
**2–3** honestly.

---

## 4. Values

The designs call out values explicitly. The recurring set: **Respect, Responsibility, Integrity,
Unity, Peace, Love, Social Justice, Patriotism.**

We do not bolt values on. Each lesson names **1–2 values** and the activity must actually create them
(Unity → genuine group task; Responsibility → real apparatus care; Integrity → honest data recording,
including anomalous results).

---

## 5. Pertinent and Contemporary Issues (PCIs)

PCIs are the cross-cutting themes. Categories seen across the Grade 10 designs:

- **Health & health promotion** — lifestyle diseases, nutrition, drug and substance use
- **Life skills** — self-esteem, assertiveness, analytical and creative thinking, decision making
- **Socio-economic & environmental issues** — environmental conservation, safety in the lab/class/school,
  global citizenship, social cohesion
- **Learner support programmes** — healthy interpersonal relationships, mentorship
- **Digital literacy & cyber security**
- **Safety and security**
- **Community involvement / service learning**

**Requirement:** at least **one PCI per lesson**, named, and honestly integrated. Chemistry's design
explicitly requires drug and substance use education in sub-strand 1.1 — that is not optional decoration.

## 6. Assessment — the four-level rubric

CBC grades by **competency level**, not percentage. Four descriptors, each with a numeric scale:

| Code | Level | Points | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **EE** | Exceeding Expectation | 4 (EE2 = 8, EE1 = 7 on the 8-point scale) | Beyond expected; applies knowledge in new, unfamiliar contexts; shows innovation |
| **ME** | Meeting Expectation | 3 (ME1 = 6, ME2 = 5) | Clear understanding; applies independently in familiar situations. **This is the target.** |
| **AE** | Approaching Expectation | 2 (AE1 = 4, AE2 = 3) | Some understanding; needs guidance; partial detail |
| **BE** | Below Expectation | 1 (BE1 = 2, BE2 = 1) | Limited understanding; needs significant support |

**Our books are scored against this. Per learning outcome we must publish a rubric row** in KICD's own
style — the official Chemistry design uses indicator rows like:

> *Ability to explain the importance of chemistry in our day to day life*
> — EE: correctly explains at least four · ME: at least three · AE: at least two · BE: at least one

Copy that **structure** (countable, observable, incremental), never the wording verbatim.

**Our positioning:** the ISOCBC series exists to move a BE/AE reader to **ME as the floor and EE as the
aim**. Every chapter therefore needs:
- **AE→ME** repair questions (the gap-fillers)
- **ME** core questions (the must-pass)
- **EE** stretch questions (unfamiliar-context application, not just "more of the same")

**Also required:** assessment is **continuous**, not only end-of-term. Each chapter needs observation
checkpoints, written tasks, a project/portfolio artefact, and a self-assessment where the learner marks
themselves against the rubric before the teacher does.

## 7. Pedagogy requirements

The designs mandate constructivist, hands-on approaches:

- **Inquiry-Based Learning** — the Key Inquiry Question genuinely drives the lesson
- **Project-Based Learning** — e.g. Chemistry 1.4 requires modelling bonding (NaCl, SiO₂, graphite,
  diamond) **using locally available materials**
- **Problem-Based Learning** — a real problem, not a dressed-up exercise

**Therefore, every chapter must ship:**
1. The **Key Inquiry Question** printed verbatim (§1.5)
2. A **hands-on activity** using materials a Kenyan school can actually obtain
3. A **project** artefact suitable for a portfolio
4. A **non-formal activity** (club, field visit, community link) per strand

**Chemistry-specific:** the design's essence statement requires **IUPAC nomenclature, terminology and
equations**. Use IUPAC names first; only note common names in parentheses.

## 8. Per-lesson compliance checklist

Tick every box before a lesson file is merged. Copy this block into the lesson's front matter.

```
LESSON: <strand>.<sub-strand> — <title>
[ ] Strand named                          [ ] Sub-strand named + lesson count
[ ] Specific Learning Outcomes quoted from the design (all of them, none invented)
[ ] Key Inquiry Question printed verbatim
[ ] Suggested Learning Experience(s) provided or improved upon
[ ] Core Competencies named (2–3) and genuinely exercised by the activity
[ ] Values named (1–2) and genuinely created
[ ] PCIs named (1+) and genuinely integrated
[ ] Activity uses locally available materials
[ ] Project / portfolio artefact included
[ ] IUPAC nomenclature used (Chemistry / Biology nomenclature for Biology)
[ ] Safety language flat, literal, imperative (if any hazard)
[ ] Rubric rows: AE / ME / EE per learning outcome
[ ] Question set tiers: AE→ME repair, ME core, EE stretch
[ ] Self-assessment checkpoint included
[ ] Fits 40 minutes of learner time
[ ] Style Guide §8 pre-ship checklist passed
[ ] Source design edition recorded (year + ISBN)
```

**Strand-level, once per strand:**
```
[ ] Strand lesson budget sums to the design's total (180 for the year)
[ ] All seven Core Competencies reached across the grade
[ ] Non-formal activity planned
[ ] End-of-strand assessment samples the design's sub-strands — nothing more, nothing less
```

## 9. Change-control when KICD revises a design

1. Download the new design PDF; record year + ISBN in the relevant `disciplines/**/STRAND_MAP.md`
   **and** in `curriculum_rules/source_mapping.json` (mark the old entry `superseded`, add the new one).
2. Diff the **Summary of Strands and Sub-Strands** table (strand names, sub-strand names, lesson counts).
3. Diff the **Specific Learning Outcomes** for every changed sub-strand.
4. Diff the **Suggested Assessment Rubric** rows.
5. Re-run §8 on every affected lesson. Changed outcome ⇒ rewrite the affected section, not just the header.
6. Open a PR titled `kicd-sync: <subject> <grade> (<year> edition)`.

---

## 10. What this checklist deliberately does NOT cover

The designs are curriculum documents; they do not dictate our tone, our page layout, our diagram style, or
our God-Mode register. **Those live in `STYLE_GUIDE.md`.** Compliance and voice are separate gates —
pass both, or the lesson does not ship.

Where the two conflict, the order of authority is:
1. **Safety** (absolute)
2. **KICD compliance** (§1–§9 of this file)
3. **Style / voice** (`STYLE_GUIDE.md`)
