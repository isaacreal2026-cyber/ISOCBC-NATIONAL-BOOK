# Junior School — English

**Tier:** KJSEA (Grade 9) · **KJSEA paper codes:** `901/1`, `901/2` (two papers)
**Status:** ⬜ Room only. No strand map, no lessons.

## Why this subject is first in the list

English is the load-bearing subject. A learner who cannot read the question cannot show what they know
in *any* other subject — including in Kiswahili, Mathematics, Integrated Science and Social Studies.
Our BE/AE reader is, more often than not, a reader whose decoding is the actual bottleneck.

**Therefore:** English content here must double as **reading-age repair** for every other subject. Write
it so that a struggling reader can survive it.

## Notes for MKUBWA

- **Never "simplify" by lowering the reading level of the *questions*.** Exam questions must be pitched
  at the real register the learner will meet. Simplify the *teaching*, not the target.
- Comprehension is the highest-yield repair area for a BE reader: it is skills-based, not
  content-based, and it transfers to every other paper.
- Build the vocabulary load explicitly. A BE learner often fails a comprehension because of three
  unknown words, not because of the idea.
- Grammar teaching must stay anchored to writing performance, not to naming parts of speech in the
  abstract.
