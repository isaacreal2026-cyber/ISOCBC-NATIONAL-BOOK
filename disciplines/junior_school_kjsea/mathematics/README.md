## Why Mathematics is the make-or-break subject

Mathematics is the single biggest placement lever in the KJSEA composite, and the subject where the
gap between a BE and an ME learner is most visible. It is also the subject where our series can move a
learner fastest, because Mathematics failure at this level is usually **a missing prerequisite**, not
missing ability.

**Core rule for this subject:** before teaching any Grade 9 topic, name and repair the Grade 6–7
prerequisite it stands on. Fractions are the classic wall — they reappear in algebra, ratio, percentage,
measurement and statistics. A learner who cannot handle fractions fails all five and blames algebra.

## Notes for MKUBWA

- **Show every step.** `STYLE_GUIDE.md` forbids "it follows that". In Mathematics this is absolute: a
  skipped step is the difference between an ME learner following and giving up.
- **Name the prerequisite at the top of every lesson.** Two lines: "Before this, you need: …". If they
  do not have it, point them to the repair ladder.
- **Teach the method mark.** CBC Mathematics rewards process. A learner who shows the right method and
  slips the arithmetic still collects marks — make that explicit, it is genuinely encouraging and true.
- **Trap discipline.** Sign errors, order of operations, unit conversion, and the fraction-of-a-quantity
  vs fraction-of-a-whole confusion deserve named traps.
- Local anchors are free here: *boda boda* fares, M-Pesa charges, shamba measurements, matatu seating,
  market prices. Use them.
