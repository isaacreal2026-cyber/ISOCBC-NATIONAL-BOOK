## Notes for MKUBWA

- Kiswahili is **not** English content with swapped vocabulary. The pedagogy, the error patterns and the
  exam traps are their own. Do not port an English lesson structure and translate it.
- **Register matters.** The exam rewards *Kiswahili sanifu*. Street register belongs in the sibling
  voice of the explanation, never inside a model answer the learner will copy.
- High-yield repair zones for a BE learner: *ngeli* (noun classes) agreement, tense marking, *uwingi*
  (pluralisation), and *ufahamu* technique.
- Build *Insha* (composition) as a repeatable scaffold, not as inspiration. BE learners gain most from
  a fixed structure they can execute under pressure.
- Cross-link with `english/` for *ufahamu* / comprehension strategy — the skill transfers, the language
  does not.
