# Senior School — STEM Pathway

**Tier:** KCBE (Grade 12) · **Grades:** 10, 11, 12 · **Cluster:** STEM
**Status:** 🟡 Chemistry and Biology strand maps exist at Grade 10. Everything else is room only.

## What a STEM learner actually studies

Senior School learners take **seven learning areas**: four compulsory — English, Kiswahili/KSL,
Community Service Learning, Physical Education — plus **three from their pathway**. At least two must
come from the chosen pathway.

The KICD Grade 10 design lists the STEM learning areas as:

Medicine-bound learners typically take Mathematics + Biology + Chemistry + Physics. Engineering and
computing tracks swap in Physics and Computer Studies. **Physics is the biggest gap in this cluster** —
it is required by most STEM careers and is not scaffolded at all.

## Lesson budget rule

**180 lessons per learning area per year**, 40 minutes each, 6 lessons per week for elective areas.
The per-sub-strand counts may flex "depending on the context", but the year total must still land on
180. See `core_directives/KICD_CHECKLIST.md` §2.

## Highest-priority open items

1. **Physics** — unscaffolded, and required by nearly every STEM career path.
2. **Grade 11 and 12** — Grades 10 is mapped; 11 and 12 do not exist yet. KCBE is sat at Grade 12, so a
   Grade 10-only series is a series with no exit exam.
3. **Verbatim verification** — the two existing Grade 10 maps carry open verification backlogs
   (Key Inquiry Questions and Specific Learning Outcomes not yet confirmed verbatim). Clear these
   before drafting.

## Notes for MKUBWA (STEM-specific)

- **IUPAC nomenclature first**, common names in parentheses only.
- Chemistry's highest safety-risk item is **preparing chlorine gas** (Grade 10, sub-strand 1.5).
  Highest safety register, teacher-present language, no jokes anywhere near it.
- Biology's most persistent BE-level error is conflating **gaseous exchange with transport**, and
  **respiration with breathing**. Kill both explicitly, more than once.
- Every practical must run on materials a real Kenyan school can obtain. Otherwise it is fiction.
